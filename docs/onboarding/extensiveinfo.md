# Parkinson's Disease Detection

Parkinson's disease is a neurological disorder that can affect a person's movement. One of the motor symptoms that clinicians assess is **bradykinesia**, which can be observed through tasks such as repetitive finger tapping.

Today, assessing these movements relies heavily on clinical observation and standardized rating scales. Our project is exploring whether **computer vision can use a simple video of a finger-tapping task to estimate motor impairment**, while also communicating when the model is uncertain about its assessment.
